Refund Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Project Milestones & Advance Deposits

To initiate a web design and development project, an initial deposit is required to reserve studio production time. If a project is cancelled prior to the delivery of initial Figma design concepts, a partial refund minus administrative onboarding costs will be processed.

2. Custom Design Sign-Off

Once design wireframes and visual mockups have been approved by the client and coding has commenced, stage milestone payments are non-refundable as dedicated engineering hours have been allocated.

3. Post-Launch Warranty

We provide complimentary bug-fixing and technical warranty (1 to 6 months depending on plan tier) to resolve any functional issues or layout discrepancies immediately following live launch.
