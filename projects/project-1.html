Apex Dental Specialty Clinic: +160% Patient Booking Surge

Redesigning a local dental clinic website to build trust, showcase treatments, and streamline appointment requests.

The Challenge

Apex Dental had an outdated, slow website built in 2018. It lacked clear pricing indicators, mobile-friendly appointment booking, and local SEO structure. As a result, prospective patients frequently called competitor clinics.

The Webora Solution

Webora built a bespoke, clean, and calming visual interface. We introduced a 2-step appointment booking widget, highlighted doctor certifications, and optimized local Google Map schema markup.
