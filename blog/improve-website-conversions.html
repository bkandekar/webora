How to Triple Your Website Conversion Rate With Strategic UX Tweaks

By Webora CRO Team • July 20, 2026 • 6 min read

Driving traffic to a website is only half the battle. If your conversion rate is 1%, doubling your traffic doubles your ad spend, but doubling your conversion rate doubles your revenue with zero extra ad spend.

1. Add Instant Floating WhatsApp Routing

For Indian and international service businesses, many customers prefer an immediate, informal WhatsApp conversation over submitting an email form and waiting 24 hours.

2. Embed Transparent Pricing Tiers

Hiding pricing behind "Contact for Quote" creates friction. Showing transparent starting prices filters out tire-kickers and builds upfront credibility with serious buyers.

3. Display Real Client Testimonials and Metrics

Social proof reduces customer anxiety. Showcase real metrics (+160% bookings, 99.9% uptime) and authentic client testimonials directly beside your call-to-action buttons.
